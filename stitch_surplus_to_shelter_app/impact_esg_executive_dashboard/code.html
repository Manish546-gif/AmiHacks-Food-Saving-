ESG & Carbon Accounting Methodology

1 rescued meal is standardized at 0.5 kg portion weight. CO2e avoidance is computed using the factor of 2.5 kg CO2e per kg food diverted from landfill methane off-gassing, complying with UNEP Food Waste Index and WRI GHG Protocol standards. All figures are audited and verified through automated digital Bluetooth smart-scales and FSSAI temperature-logging telemetry at point of courier handover.
