## Slide 2: Problem Statement
### The Problem We Are Solving

- India generates millions of tons of food waste every year, while millions of people still face hunger and food insecurity.
- Restaurants, caterers, messes, and grocery stores often discard edible surplus food due to a lack of a timely redistribution channel.
- Existing food donation workflows are fragmented, manual, and unreliable.
- Critical challenges include:
  - Short expiry windows for prepared food
  - Lack of trust and verification between donors and recipients
  - Difficulty matching surplus food to nearby shelters or kitchens
  - Limited visibility and tracking of real-time delivery status
  - No structured system for accountability, receipts, and impact reporting

### Impact
- Food wastage increases the environmental burden
- Socially vulnerable groups remain underserved
- Operational inefficiency slows down donation processes

---

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution
### Our Solution

Surplus-to-Shelter is a web-based platform that connects food donors with verified recipient shelters and volunteer drivers.

- Donors can quickly list surplus food with details like quantity, type, expiry time, and packaging.
- The system automatically identifies the best matching shelter based on location, capacity, and needs.
- Verified shelters can accept or reject food offers in real time.
- Volunteer drivers are assigned to collect and deliver the food efficiently.
- The platform ensures transparency through live tracking, OTP-based verification, and digital receipts.

### Key Objective
Reduce food waste while improving last-mile delivery to communities in need.

## Slide 4: System Architecture
### System Architecture

**Frontend**
- React + Vite web application
- Role-based dashboards for donors, shelters, drivers, and admins

**Backend / Logic Layer**
- Donation intake and matching logic
- Verification checks and offer acceptance flow
- Driver assignment and route coordination

**Data Layer**
- Donor, recipient, driver, and donation records
- Real-time status updates and notifications

**Mapping & Tracking**
- Location-based matching and route visualization
- Live monitoring of pickup and delivery

**Admin Panel**
- Oversight of all food offers and system health
- Manual intervention when required

### Architecture Flow
Donor posts food → Offer broadcast → Shelter accepts → Driver assigned → Pickup → Delivery → Receipt generated

## Slide 5: Key Features
